Word lists

AI word filtering

A word list is only as good as the words you would actually use. This teaches an AI how you judge fill — in your words, not ours — and then puts it to work on the rest of the list. It is separate from the training on the Word lists page, which learns from your swipes and runs entirely on your own machine.

What this does

  1. You write the rules. What you keep, what you cut, what a score means. An example is there to edit or throw away.
  2. You correct it on a hundred words, spread across the whole list: familiar and obscure, single words and phrases, published and never published.
  3. It rewrites your rules to match the decisions you actually made, and shows you what it changed.
  4. Then it judges words you have not seen, the same way you would. Another hundred sharpens it.
